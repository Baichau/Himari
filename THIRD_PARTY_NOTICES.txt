Bundled Faster-Whisper speech model
===================================

an OpenAI Whisper model. This tested build includes the English-only base.en
model:
https://huggingface.co/openai/whisper-base.en

https://huggingface.co/openai/whisper-large-v3-turbo

Bundled English Piper voice
===========================

The en_US-amy-medium voice model is distributed separately from Himari's
application code. Its model card identifies the MycroftAI Mimic 3 voices
project as its dataset source and says it was fine-tuned from the
en_US-lessac-medium voice:
https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/tree/main/en/en_US/amy/medium

The Mimic 3 voices project is licensed under Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0):
https://github.com/MycroftAI/mimic3-voices
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

The upstream Amy model and its configuration are included unchanged. The
voice files remain under their applicable upstream license; this notice does
not apply that license to Himari's separate application code.

An alternate en_US-lessac-medium Piper voice may be bundled when it is the
only complete local English voice available. Its voice model is distributed
under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA
4.0). The upstream model card and files are available at:
https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/tree/main/en/en_US/lessac/medium
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
